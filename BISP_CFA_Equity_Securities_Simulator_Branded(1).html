    IAS / IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Important distinction: “Overview of Equity Securities” is an investment-analysis topic, not an IFRS or US GAAP compliance module. Accounting rules matter because they determine how issuers classify, present, measure, and disclose equity instruments and how investors account for equity holdings.
    AreaIFRS / IASUS GAAPConnection to CFA equity analysis
    Issuer classification: liability vs equityIAS 32ASC 480 and other instrument-specific guidanceLegal form alone is not enough; contractual obligations can affect whether an instrument is presented as equity or liability.
    Investor accounting for equity investmentsIFRS 9ASC 321 and related guidanceMeasurement of equity holdings affects reported gains/losses but does not change the economic ownership characteristics described in CFA.
    Financial-instrument disclosuresIFRS 7ASC 825 and instrument-specific disclosuresDisclosures help analysts evaluate the significance and risks of financial instruments.
    Fair valueIFRS 13ASC 820Market prices can support fair-value measurement of quoted equity securities.
    Book equity / presentationIFRS 18 / other applicable IFRS presentation requirementsUS GAAP equity presentation guidanceBook equity is an accounting measure; market value reflects investor pricing and expectations.
