    IAS / IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Important distinction: Company forecasting is an analyst technique, not a separate IFRS or US GAAP standard. Accounting standards matter because historical reported data and disclosures form the starting point for the forecast.
    Forecast AreaIFRS / IASUS GAAPForecasting Relevance
    RevenueIFRS 15ASC 606Historical revenue recognition and disaggregation inform volume, price, segment, and contract assumptions.
    Operating segmentsIFRS 8ASC 280Segment disclosures can support separate forecasts for products, geographies, and business units.
    InventoryIAS 2ASC 330Inventory accounting affects historical COGS, margins, and days-based working-capital assumptions.
    PPE / capital investmentIAS 16 / IAS 36ASC 360Capex, depreciation, and impairment history informs PPE forecasts.
    Income taxesIAS 12ASC 740Historical effective tax rates, deferred tax positions, and jurisdiction mix inform tax assumptions.
    PresentationIFRS 18 effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 Jan 2027US GAAP presentation + SEC requirements for registrantsConsistent classification and subtotals improve historical-to-forecast comparability.
