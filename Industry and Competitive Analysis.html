    IAS / IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Important distinction: Industry and competitive analysis is an investment-analysis framework, not an IFRS or US GAAP compliance topic. Accounting standards matter because analysts use reported revenue, segment, margin, asset and disclosure data to measure industry structure and competitive position.
    Analysis AreaIFRS / IASUS GAAPWhy It Matters
    Operating segmentsIFRS 8ASC 280Segment disclosures help define business activities, geographies, products and customer concentrations.
    RevenueIFRS 15ASC 606Comparable revenue recognition supports market-size and market-share analysis.
    Financial instruments / fair valueIFRS 9 / IFRS 13ASC 320/321 / ASC 820Relevant where industry profitability or valuation relies on financial assets or market-value data.
    PPE and asset intensityIAS 16 / IAS 36ASC 360Asset-heavy industries can have different barriers to entry, cost structures and return characteristics.
    Presentation and disclosuresIFRS 18 from periods beginning 1 Jan 2027; other current IFRS presentation requirementsUS GAAP presentation + SEC requirements for registrantsConsistent presentation improves peer comparisons, margin analysis and competitive benchmarking.
