Common-Size

Scale CFO, CFI, CFF, capex or free cash flow to revenue to compare periods or firms.

FCFF

Cash flow available to debt and equity capital providers after operating and reinvestment needs.

FCFE

Cash flow available to common equity holders after capital investment and net borrowing effects.

Ratios

Evaluate cash profitability, earnings quality, reinvestment burden and debt/interest coverage.

    Important distinction

    FCFF, FCFE and many cash flow ratios are analytical measures used in CFA analysis; they are not standard IFRS or US GAAP line items. This simulator therefore keeps reported-accounting standards separate from analyst calculations.
