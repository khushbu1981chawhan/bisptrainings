Private Equity Fundamentals — Topic Map

Eight core areas covered in this simulator.

01

Definition

Private ownership, buyouts, venture capital, growth equity, active ownership, and exit-driven returns.

02

Characteristics

Illiquidity, long horizons, valuation uncertainty, manager dispersion, fees, and appraisal smoothing.

03

Investment lifecycle

Sourcing, screening, diligence, acquisition, value creation, monitoring, and exit.

04

GP/LP structure

Manager responsibilities, investor commitments, LPA terms, governance, alignment, and LPAC oversight.

05

Fund commitment

Committed, contributed, unfunded capital, liquidity planning, overcommitment, and default remedies.

06

Capital calls

Drawdowns, notices, uses of capital, unfunded balance, subscription lines, and liquidity risk.

07

Distributions

Exit proceeds, DPI, RVPI, TVPI, waterfalls, carry, and irregular cash-return timing.

08

Fund termination

Wind-down, extensions, final realizations, clawback, final accounting, and dissolution.

About BISP Trainings

BISP Trainings focuses on practical, scenario-driven learning. The CFA simulator is designed to convert curriculum concepts into repeatable exam skills through structured learning, worked examples, progressive questioning, diagnostics, and revision planning.

  • Concept-to-question learning
  • Progressive difficulty
  • Performance analytics
  • Weak-area diagnosis
  • Personalized study planning

BISP CFA Learning Philosophy

Learn → Apply → Test → Diagnose → Revise → Retest.

This cycle ensures study time is allocated based on evidence rather than treating every topic equally.
