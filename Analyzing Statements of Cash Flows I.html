    IAS / IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Cash-Flow AreaIFRS / IASUS GAAPCFA analytical point
    Core cash-flow statementIAS 7ASC 230Operating, investing and financing categories reconcile cash between balance-sheet dates.
    Operating methodIAS 7 permits direct or indirect presentationASC 230 permits direct or indirect presentation, with specific reconciliation requirementsCandidates should prepare and convert between methods.
    Noncash investing/financingExcluded from cash-flow totals and disclosed separatelySimilarly disclosed outside cash-flow totalsDo not force noncash transactions into CFI/CFF totals.
    Interest/dividendsIAS 7 classification requirements have historically differed from US GAAP; IFRS 18 amends IAS 7 for periods beginning 2027Interest paid and interest/dividends received generally operating; dividends paid financingNormalize classifications when comparing cross-framework companies.
    Supplier financeIAS 7 and IFRS 7 include supplier-finance disclosure amendmentsUS GAAP has its own supplier-finance disclosure requirementsFinancing-like working-capital arrangements can affect interpretation.

    Current standards awareness

    IAS 7 remains the core IFRS cash-flow standard. IFRS 18, effective for annual periods beginning on or after 1 January 2027, amended aspects of IAS 7 including the indirect-method starting point and interest/dividend classification requirements. The IASB also continues a separate project on potential future improvements to the statement of cash flows.
