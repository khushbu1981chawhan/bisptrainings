Spreadsheet Formula References

Number of Orders = ROUND(Annual Usage / Order Quantity, 0)
Total Variable Costs = Number of Orders × Variable Cost per Order
Average Inventory = ROUND(Order Quantity / 2, 0)
Total Holding Costs = Average Inventory × Unit Price × Holding Cost %
Total Inventory Costs = Total Holding Costs + Total Variable Costs
Cost of Goods = Annual Usage × Unit Price
Total Costs = Cost of Goods + Total Inventory Costs
Saving / Cost of Supplier Policy = EOQ Total Cost − Supplier Total Cost


  

    Concept: Inventory policy cost combines ordering cost and holding cost. A smaller order quantity normally increases the number of orders but reduces average inventory. The model compares both policies to show which produces the lower total annual cost.
