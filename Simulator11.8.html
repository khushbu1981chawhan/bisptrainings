Spreadsheet Formula References

Year 2 Fixed Costs = B4 × (1 + $B$8)
Year 3 Fixed Costs = C4 × (1 + $B$8)
Year 2 Variable Cost = B5 × (1 + $B$9)
Year 3 Variable Cost = C5 × (1 + $B$9)
Year 2 Selling Price = B6 × (1 + $B$10)
Year 3 Selling Price = C6 × (1 + $B$10)
Contribution = Selling Price − Variable Cost
Break-even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution


  

    Concept: The worksheet uses absolute references for growth assumptions and relative references for year-to-year values. This makes the formula reusable across future years without rewriting the logic.
