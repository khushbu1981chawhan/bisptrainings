    IAS / IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Important distinction: Market efficiency is an investment-market concept, not an IFRS or US GAAP compliance requirement. Accounting standards matter because public financial reports, fair-value measures, and risk disclosures are part of the information set that market participants process.
    AreaIFRS / IASUS GAAPMarket-efficiency connection
    Financial instrument disclosuresIFRS 7ASC 825 and instrument-specific disclosuresTransparent disclosures improve the public information set available to investors.
    Financial instrument classification & measurementIFRS 9ASC 320 / ASC 321 and related guidanceReported carrying values and gains/losses can affect the information investors process.
    Fair valueIFRS 13ASC 820Quoted market prices and market-participant assumptions connect reporting to observable market information.
    Financial statement presentationIAS 1 currently; IFRS 18 effective for annual periods beginning 1 Jan 2027US GAAP presentation + SEC requirements for registrantsComparable presentation can support faster interpretation of public information.
    Earnings per shareIAS 33ASC 260EPS is a widely used public data point; market efficiency concerns how quickly prices incorporate such information, not how EPS is calculated.
