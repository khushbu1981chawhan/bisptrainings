    IFRS / US GAAP Connections

    Important distinction: Security market index construction itself is not governed by IFRS or US GAAP. Index methodology is set by index providers. Accounting standards become relevant to how index constituents and index-linked financial instruments are measured and reported by entities.
    AreaIFRSUS GAAPConnection
    Financial assets held by investorsIFRS 9 classification and measurementASC 320 / ASC 321 and related guidanceAccounting treatment may affect reported values of securities that happen to be index constituents.
    Fair valueIFRS 13ASC 820Quoted prices in active markets can be relevant to fair-value measurement; IFRS 13 defines fair value as an exit price.
    Financial-instrument disclosuresIFRS 7ASC 825 and instrument-specific requirementsDisclosures help users understand the significance and risks of financial instruments.
    Index-linked investmentsIFRS 9 / other instrument-specific standards depending on structureInstrument-specific US GAAPIndex funds, derivatives, and structured products may have accounting consequences separate from how the underlying index is constructed.
