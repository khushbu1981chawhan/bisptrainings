Core Formula

P = Σ [C / (1 + y/m)^t] + FV / (1 + y/m)^N

P = bond price, C = coupon per period, y = annual YTM, m = coupon frequency, N = number of remaining coupon periods.

For between-coupon-date valuation, full price includes accrued interest; quoted clean price excludes it.

Finance vs Accounting

CFA valuation asks what cash flows are worth given a required yield. Financial reporting asks how the investment should be classified, measured and presented in the financial statements. The topics overlap, but they are not the same calculation.
